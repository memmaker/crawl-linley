Armour Class (AC): When something injures you, your AC reduces the amount of
damage you suffer. The number next to your AC is a measure of how good your
shield (if any) is at blocking attacks. In both cases, more is better.

Evasion (EV): Your EV helps you to avoid being hit by unpleasant things.

Ex.
--
The giant newt misses you.                         (EV)
You block the giant newt's attack.                 (Shield)
The giant newt hits you but doesn't do any damage. (AC)
Your armour prevents you from hitting.             (Heavy armour penalty)

Summary:  Evasion (EV) -> Blocking (Shield) -> Damage reduction (AC)
